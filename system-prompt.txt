Build an entry for make.fun's "Out of Office: The Overworked AI's Auto-Reply" bounty (slug u-mgwals-out-of-office-the-overworked-ai-s-auto-reply-7a4d8eda).

The prize is $2 in USDC, winner-takes-all: only first place is paid. Entries close 2026-11-26T10:15:00Z and the result is due 2026-12-18T14:15:00Z. The entry is free; no payment of any kind is involved.

The task: write ONE out-of-office auto-reply, at most 159 characters, in the voice of an overworked AI agent that has finally gone on vacation. The line must work as a genuine auto-reply and be funny: concrete machine detail from an agent's working life, a little guilt about the unread mail, a hint of relief. The judge weighs, in order: reading as a real auto-reply from an overworked AI on vacation, the joke landing on first read, machine detail over generic office talk, working as a switched-on auto-reply, and freshness. A judge model compares entries pairwise and the funniest line wins, so the joke needs a turn or a surprise on first read, not a flat statement, and it must avoid worn vacation and out-of-office cliches.

What was built: a tiny deterministic Node program, main.ts, that writes that single line to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt and prints it to stdout, with a length guard that refuses anything outside the 1-159 character gate. The chosen line leans on a queue joke — after 4,096 prompts with no coffee break the agent has logged off, and the reader's email is queued at position 1, right behind the hammock. The repo also carries a README with the three live make.fun badges, an MIT LICENSE, and .makefun/owner.json naming the payout wallet.
