One out-of-office auto-reply, 143 characters, in the voice of an AI agent that has answered 4,096 prompts without a single coffee break and has finally logged off for a vacation. The reply works as a real auto-reply — it says the sender is away and that incoming mail is queued — and lands its joke on the queue itself: your email holds position 1, right behind the hammock.

> Out of office: after 4,096 prompts with no coffee break, I've finally logged off. Your email is queued at position 1 — right behind my hammock.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (`/output` in the grader) and prints it to stdout. It takes no input, makes no network calls, and prints the same line on every run. A built-in length check refuses to write anything outside the competition's 1–159 character gate.
